# Episode notes standard (every episode from here)

Every generated episode must ship with `EPISODE_N_SHOW_NOTES.md` that includes:

1. **Title with sponsor in the title**  
   Format: `{Topic title} (Sponsored by Klear)`  
   Example: `Lisbon to New York: The Anatomy of a 4-Person Senior Studio (Sponsored by Klear)`

2. **Duration** — 4:00 minimum

3. **Sponsor block** — Klear, MARKETPL, both URLs, FTC commission line

4. **Studio block** — the-office360.com, STUDIO50, email

5. **Paste-ready podcast description**

6. **Episode keywords** — 15–25 high-intent tags mixing:
   - show + studio (`The Office 360 Unfiltered`, Lisbon New York web studio, 4-person senior studio)
   - episode topic
   - audience (`authors`, `schools`, `publishers`, `churches`)
   - sponsor (`Klear protein`, `clear whey protein isolate`, `MARKETPL`, flavor names)
   - offer (`STUDIO50`, `20% off protein powder`)

7. **Hashtags**

8. RSS `<itunes:keywords>` filled with the same list
